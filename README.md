# Dark Deco

Two-player co-op prototype: a foggy art deco city seen from the rooftops, with Hyper Light Drifter-style dash and combat.

Play: https://hwezyrmas.github.io/dark-deco/

- One phone taps **Host a game** and shares the four-letter code; the other types it in and taps **Join**.
- Classes: **Warden** (protector: greatsword, Bash, Dome) and **Lamplighter** (healer: lantern twirl, Step, Flare). Flare inside the Dome makes a burning sanctuary.
- Practice alone with a bot from the menu.
- The first prototype (Bulwark + Archer, top-down) is kept at `/v1/`.
- Turn sound on. Works over Wi-Fi or mobile data. Phones connect directly through PeerJS (`peerjs.min.js`, v1.5.4, bundled).
