Feature · The Protector

Fury in Two Axes

He does the killing and takes the hits. She makes sure he gets back up.

By our rooftop correspondent · 140 health

Nobody on the rooftops asks the Warden to slow down. He fights up close with two axes, and every blow feeds a red bar of Rage under his health. When the swarm closes in, that Rage pours out as Whirlwind; when it fills to the brim, he becomes a storm.

His job is simple: stand between the dark and the Lamplighter. He has the health for it, and two ways to fight: the Berserker, all axes and Rage, or the Executioner, one greataxe that finishes anything weak.

“She keeps me standing. I keep the dark off her.”

The Girl Who Carries the Light

Fragile, a little slow, and the only reason he is still alive.

By our society columnist · 90 health

She will never out-fight him, and she is not trying to. The Lamplighter fires bolts of lantern light that heal the Warden as they pass through him, leaves pools of light wherever she steps, and keeps a Solar Lance ready for the moment he staggers.

Stay behind him, Ward him from the big hits, and mark the crowd for him to blow up. Healer keeps him alive; Firebrand sets everything on fire.

“I don't need to win the fight. I need him to.”

Breaking news

The roofs fight back

Red on the floor means move.

Brutes, bombers, Gatekeepers and the Foreman now warn before they hit: a red circle or a lane of arrows fills up, then lands. It hits hard. From 0:45 falling stone also targets wherever you are heading. Standing still is how heroes die.

Fight to the beat.

The rooftops run on a 120 bpm beat you can hear in the music. Each hero does one thing per beat: the next big ability in a fixed loop, in the order you learned them, or a basic attack when that ability is still cooling down. The glowing icon in your skill bar is firing now, the outlined one is next, and the dots under your hero show the whole loop. A ring closes in on your hero just before the next one fires: that is your moment to step into the crowd. Her beats fall between his, so together you alternate.

Basic attacks come in threes.

Every spec's basic attack runs a 3-step cycle, shown by three pips under your hero. Berserker: forehand, lunging backhand, healing overhead slam. Executioner: long chop, wide sweep, heavy drop. Healer: one bolt, twin bolts, a five-bolt flare. Firebrand: one cinder, two cinders, a firebomb that leaves the ground burning. Stop attacking for 4 seconds and the cycle starts over.

You aim the big spells.

The chevrons in front of your hero show where you are facing. Big abilities only fire at enemies in that direction, so turn toward the crowd. When the chevrons pulse, a spell is waiting for a target.

Chase the light.

Gems fade if you leave them. Hearts are the only pickup that heals. Gold altars appear on the map: stand inside to charge them for healing, power and XP. Twin altars only charge while you hold both at once. Guarded chests sit behind brutes.

Stay close.

Her healing barely reaches him outside the dashed ring. Enemies go for whoever is weaker, so the Warden has to stand in their way. Every time you go down, getting back up takes longer.

Special report

When the bridges burn

The Divide.

Twice a night, at 4:50 and again at 8:35, the Foreman sets the bridges on fire. The bridges flash red for three seconds, then the Warden is pulled to the main roof and the Lamplighter to the east roof. For 35 seconds you fight alone: no healing reaches across, and the shadows on each roof hunt only the hero standing there.

Not without you.

When the bridges burn, each of you carries one Memory of the other, shown by the glowing icon with two reaching hands (HER for him, HIM for her). You never press it. If your partner would fall while you are apart, your Memory spends itself: they hold on at 30% health, cannot be hurt for 2 seconds, and a burst of light throws the shadows off them. One save each per Divide, gone once you are together again.

Find each other.

When the fire dies, an arrow points to your partner. Cross a bridge and meet up: Reunited heals you both, blasts the crowd away and Empowers you for 8 seconds. If you go down while apart, you stay down instead of being knocked out, so your partner can lift you up after the reunion.

Hold until dawn.

The Foreman arrives at 11:00. Bring him down for the best ending, or simply survive: at 12:00 the sun rises and burns him away. If a phone locks or switches app, the run pauses until both of you are back.

Advice column

Ask the Lamplighter

“My Warden keeps falling over. What am I doing wrong?”

You are probably in front of him. Stay behind him, and throw a Ward over him whenever red appears under his feet. If he does go down, stand next to him to bring him back before the 20 seconds run out.

“When should I pick a passive instead of a new ability?”

Once you have four or five abilities. Every ability's level 5 evolution needs a specific passive, and each entry in the Arsenal tells you which one.

“What's in the gold chests?”

Gatekeepers drop them. Grab one for an evolution, a duo or a handful of free upgrades.

“Too many numbers on screen!”

Every hit shows its number by default. You can switch to big hits only in the Learned skills screen.
