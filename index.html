Feature · The Protector

Fury in Two Axes

He does the killing and takes the hits. She makes sure he gets back up.

By our rooftop correspondent · 140 health

Nobody on the rooftops asks the Warden to slow down. He fights up close with two axes, and every blow feeds a red bar of Rage under his health. When the swarm closes in, that Rage pours out as Whirlwind; when it fills to the brim, he becomes a storm.

His job is simple: stand between the dark and the Lamplighter. He has the health for it, the Dome to shelter them both, and a leap that lands like a hammer.

“She keeps me standing. I keep the dark off her.”

Profile · The Healer

The Girl Who Carries the Light

Fragile, a little slow, and the only reason he is still alive.

By our society columnist · 90 health

She will never out-fight him, and she is not trying to. The Lamplighter fires bolts of lantern light that heal the Warden as they pass through him, leaves pools of light wherever she steps, and keeps a Solar Lance ready for the moment he staggers.

Stay behind him, keep the light on him, and let him do the rest. Fire the Lance through the Dome and it splits into a rainbow.

“I don't need to win the fight. I need him to.”

Advice column

Ask the Lamplighter

“My Warden keeps falling over. What am I doing wrong?”

You are probably in front of him. Stay behind, keep your bolts flying through him, and save the Lance for when his bar turns red. If he does go down, stand next to him to bring him back before the 20 seconds run out.

“When should I pick a passive instead of a new ability?”

Once you have four or five abilities. Every ability's level 5 evolution needs a specific passive, and each entry in the Arsenal tells you which one.

“What's in the gold chests?”

Gatekeepers drop them. Grab one for an evolution, a duo or a handful of free upgrades.

“Too many numbers on screen!”

Only big hits show by default. You can switch all of them on in the Learned skills screen.
