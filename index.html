Meet the heroes

Protector · 140 healthWarden

A berserker with two axes. Get in close and let the axes work while she stays behind you.

Automatic

Fury Strikes: a 3-hit axe combo on anything near you. Every hit builds Rage, the red bar under your health.

Rage

Whirlwind spends Rage on its own when enemies surround you. At full Rage the small button turns into STORM: tap it for Bladestorm, a long spin you can't be hurt in.

Small button · Q

Heroic Leap: jump the way you're moving and slam down on a crowd. You can't be hurt in the air.

Big button · E

Dome: a shield you can both stand in. Enemies are pushed out, bullets stop at its edge, and you take less damage inside.

Good picks

Iron Skin, Ravager, War Banner and Armor. You're the wall, so take the hits for her.

Healer · 90 healthLamplighter

Fragile but deadly from a distance. Keep moving, stay behind the Warden and keep him alive.

Automatic

Light Bolts: shoots the nearest enemy, faster the longer you keep firing. Bolts that fly through the Warden heal him.

Small button · Q

Step: a quick blink in the direction you're moving. It leaves a pool of light that heals whoever stands in it.

Big button · E

Solar Lance: a huge beam. Drag the button to aim, or just tap and it aims itself. Through your partner it heals 30% and powers them up; through the Dome it splits into a rainbow.

Good picks

Glow Garden, Sun Kite, Halo Pulse and Regeneration help you both. Star Fall and Prism hit hardest.

Bond

Stay close and the gold Bond bar fills: you both hit harder, attack faster and slowly heal. Drift apart and it fades. Coming back together after being apart sets off a Rekindle blast.

Level ups

Experience is shared, and you each pick your own card. You hold up to 6 abilities and 4 passives. Level 3 of an ability adds a new effect; level 5 evolves it if you own the passive the card names.

Duo abilities

When you each own the right pair of abilities, a duo card can appear, like Ember Storm (Whirlwind + Fireflies). Duos only work while you stand close.

Treasure

Gatekeepers drop a gold chest. Grab it for an evolution, a duo or bonus upgrades.

Going down

If one of you falls, the other stands beside them to revive. Wait too long and they're out for 20 seconds.
