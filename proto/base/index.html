DARK DECO

Base defense demo

The city has gone quiet and green. Your generator is the last light on this roof. Keep it running through the night.

By day pick a building at the bottom, then tap the ground to place it. Red markers show where tonight's horde comes from.

At night drag anywhere to move. The Warden swings on his own. Tap Leap to slam into a crowd.

Arc Pylons zap the dead. Lamps slow them and make them take more damage. Spikes wear down with use.

Survive three nights. If the generator falls, you lose part of the base and try the night again.
