Shared systems

Beat loop
120 bpm. Big abilities fire in a fixed rotation, one per beat, in learned order; long cooldowns skip slots until ready. Lamplighter offset by half a beat. Auto attacks snap to the beat grid: axe combo and bolts on half-beats, greataxe and cinders on beats.
Marks
Her hits mark enemies (4s). His next hit on a marked enemy detonates it (credited to him); 1.5s before it can be re-marked.
Aiming
Big targeted abilities only pick enemies inside a 104° cone in the direction you are moving.
Telegraphs
Brute slam, bomber blast and fire puddle, Gatekeeper slam and charge lane, Foreman bullet rings and stone rain, falling masonry from 0:45.
Heal reach
Her healing does 25% outside 115 px of her.
Downs
Each time you go down, revives take longer and return less health.
Roaming
Gems fade after 16s; hearts are the only heal pickup; gold altars (stand in), twin altars (hold both), guarded chests.
