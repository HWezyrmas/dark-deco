# Playtest script (roadmap Phase 1)

One session = one pair of players, about 45 minutes. Aim for 3 to 5 sessions, at least 2 with people who have never seen the game.

## Before
- Two phones, sound on, charged. Both open https://hwezyrmas.github.io/dark-deco/ (add to home screen if possible).
- Use the testers' own phones when you can; note the models.
- Have `FINDINGS.md` open (copy it per session) or a notes app.
- Your role is to **watch, not teach**. Don't explain the controls or mechanics unless they're stuck for more than a minute. Every time you have to explain something, write it down: that's a finding.

## 1. First run (15 min): cold start
- Say only: "It's a two-player game. One of you hosts, the other joins with the code. Try to survive until dawn."
- Let them pick heroes and specs themselves.
- Watch for:
  - Do they understand what to do in the first minute?
  - Do they talk to each other? What do they say?
  - Do they notice when the partner is down, and go to revive?
  - What happens at the Divide (bridges burn)? Do they understand they're split, and do they find each other after?
  - Does Not Without You trigger? Do they notice it saved them?
  - Do they read the level-up cards or tap the first one?
  - Any moment they lose track of their own hero in the effects?
  - The boss: do they see its attacks coming?
- When the run ends, don't comment. Watch whether they want to go again without being asked.

## 2. Second run (15 min): swap
- Swap heroes (or specs) if they're willing. Same observation list.
- Note what changed: faster, more teamwork, still confused about the same things?

## 3. Base defense prototype (5 to 10 min)
- Menu, "Base defense prototype" link (one phone at a time; it's single player for now).
- Ask them to place a few buildings and survive one night.
- Watch: do they understand building by tapping? Do they plan around the red gates? Do they want to keep playing?

## 4. Five questions (5 min)
Ask each player separately if possible, write their exact words:
1. In one sentence, what is this game?
2. What was the best moment?
3. What was the most confusing or annoying moment?
4. Did your partner matter, or could you have played alone?
5. Would you play another run right now? Would you pay for it?

## After
- On the host phone: menu, **Copy test log**. Paste it to Claude together with your notes (the log holds every run on that phone; Claude reads the newest ones).
- Fill one `FINDINGS.md` per session, or just paste raw notes and let Claude sort them.
